01

Our commitment

Promote an inclusive, equitable workplace free from discrimination, where all employees feel valued and respected.

02

Diversity and inclusion

Statement of commitment

DVGenesis promotes diversity and inclusion in all its practices and policies.

Training and awareness

All employees receive regular training on diversity, equity and inclusion.

Code of Conduct

Our code of conduct includes clear guidelines on inclusive behavior and respect for diversity.

03

Recruitment and selection

Fair selection process

Recruitment and selection processes are fair and impartial, using objective criteria.

Candidate diversity

We promote diversity in our candidate pool, actively seeking talent from different backgrounds and experiences.

Recruiter training

We provide recruiters with specific training on unconscious bias and inclusive practices.

04

Talent development and retention

Career plans

We develop career plans that consider diversity and offer equal growth opportunities to all employees.

Mentoring and coaching

We have mentoring and coaching programs to support the development of diverse talent.

Performance reviews

We ensure performance reviews are fair and based on objective criteria.

05

Inclusive workplace

Accessibility

We ensure the workplace is accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities, when work is carried out in person.

Flexibility

We offer flexible working policies that meet the needs of different employee groups.

Affinity groups

We have affinity groups to support employees from different backgrounds and promote inclusion.

06

Monitoring and evaluation

DEI indicators

We establish indicators to monitor the progress of DEI initiatives.

Ongoing feedback

We regularly collect employee feedback on DEI initiatives and make adjustments as needed.

Annual reports

We publish annual reports on the progress and results of DEI initiatives.

07

Communication and transparency

Internal communication

We maintain open and transparent communication about DEI policies and initiatives.

Leadership engagement

We ensure the company's leadership is engaged with and actively supports DEI initiatives.

Events and campaigns

We organize events and campaigns to promote awareness of and engagement with DEI topics.
